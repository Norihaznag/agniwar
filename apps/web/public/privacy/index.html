Privacy Policy

This policy describes the information used by agniwar to connect devices and transfer files. Questions about privacy can be sent to support@agniwar.com.

Discovery and technical information

The signaling service can see your network IP address, browser-provided user agent, temporary device identifier and friendly name, connection times, and connection setup messages, including WebRTC candidates. It uses that information to coordinate connections and device availability. The hosting and network providers may process or log request metadata; their retention practices depend on their configuration.

Devices using the same public IP address may see one another’s temporary device names and types. This does not verify that they share a Wi-Fi network or establish their identities.

Files and storage

File names, sizes and types are shared with the recipient in the transfer request. File contents are sent only after the recipient accepts, over an encrypted peer connection; the signaling server does not store or process them. A configured TURN provider may relay encrypted traffic and see connection metadata.

Verified received files may be kept in browser storage for downloading again, until you remove them, clear site data or the browser evicts them. An interruption may leave temporary site data. If you choose a folder or download a file, that copy is stored separately on your device; removing a history entry or clearing browser data does not remove that separate copy.

Local settings and third-party services

agniwar stores your theme preference locally in your browser and uses browser storage for received-file history and the application shell. It does not require an account. Hosting, signaling and any configured STUN or TURN providers process the technical information needed to provide their services.

Your choices

You can remove entries in Received files and clear the site’s browser storage through your browser settings. Browser settings can also restrict local storage, which may limit functionality. For privacy questions or applicable requests, contact support@agniwar.com.
