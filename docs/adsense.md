# Preparing AGNIWAR for AdSense

Google reviews sites and accounts; no design, template or checklist can guarantee approval. This project does not include an AdSense publisher ID, ad scripts, ad slots or a consent manager. The `/transfer` application remains ad-free. The guides and information pages are accessible without an account and have ordinary navigation, but the operator must review, improve and maintain their content before applying.

Before applying or serving ads:

1. Deploy on a real, publicly accessible HTTPS domain. Set `PUBLIC_SITE_URL` to that origin and `PUBLIC_CONTACT_EMAIL` to a genuine support address you control. Check the canonical URLs, sitemap, robots.txt, crawlability and all links.
2. Review each article for original, useful and accurate content. Expand it with real experience and keep it current. Verify the operator's About, Contact, Privacy and Terms pages; the included legal text is a starting point, not legal advice.
3. Follow the current Google Publisher and AdSense policies, including content, prohibited practices, ad placement and privacy disclosures. Do not place ads in the transfer workflow or disguise ads as controls.
4. If serving personalized ads to users in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, review Google's consent requirements and use an appropriate Google-certified CMP integrated with the IAB TCF. Review applicable consent and privacy rules for every region served. Update your privacy policy to reflect your actual ad providers and practices.
5. Apply through your own AdSense account. Only add real publisher/slot IDs, ads.txt and reviewed ad integration when appropriate for that account and deployment. Never invent IDs or imply that the site has already been approved.
